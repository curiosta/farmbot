Satellite snapshot · 2.2 km box

Green now, brown in March

Across a 2.2 km box around the village point, mean NDVI was 0.77 on 21 Oct 2026 (96% of pixels above 0.5), against 0.37 on 12 Mar 2026 (18%). 74% of the box gains more than 0.3 NDVI between the dry season and the monsoon. That is the signature of a single rain-fed kharif crop. Satellite-observed
