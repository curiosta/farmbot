Nilabadi village

How the paddy around Nilabadi is doing this week, what pests and weather to watch for, the village in numbers, and where to get help.

This week in Nilabadi

The crop

Most paddy around Nilabadi is still green; harvest hasn't started.

On the latest clear satellite photo (23 Oct 2026), about 269 ha of paddy was still green, about 3 ha was turning golden and almost none was cut.

Pests and diseases

High risk: blast (leaf and neck). Watch for: sheath blight.

Walk the field and compare with the photos in Spot the pest. These alerts come from the weather, so ask KVK Koraput before spraying. Nilabadi pest alerts →

Weather

Heavy rain is forecast in parts of the village on Wed 04 Nov (78 mm) and Thu 05 Nov (91 mm). Drier days: 10 Nov–13 Nov.

Nilabadi at a glance

  • 2,123 people in 476 households.
  • 511 ha of village land (about 1,263 acres).
  • About 416 ha sown with crops (about 1,028 acres), and about 173 ha watered by canal (about 427 acres).
  • Main crops: paddy, then lemon grass and cotton.
  • Gram panchayat: Neelabadi, in Bandhugaon block, Koraput district.

Source: Census of India 2011 village records, via Odiapedia.

The land from above

About half of the land around the village is crop fields, nearly a third is trees and about a fifth is grass and open land. Houses and roads cover only a small part.

Source: Sentinel-2 satellite photos (European Space Agency); land map ESA WorldCover 2021. How we made this →

Where to get help

  • A pest, a disease or a sick patch: KVK Koraput, the farm science centre at Sunabeda, +91-7008930482.
  • Free farmer helplines: Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551 (answered in Odia) and Krushi Samrudhi 155333.
  • Crop insurance (PMFBY): report crop loss within 72 hours on 14447.
