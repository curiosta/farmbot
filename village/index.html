One-time satellite look · a 2.2 km square

Green now, brown in March

FarmBot looked at a 2.2 km square around the village. On 21 Oct 2026 the average greenness score was 0.77, and 96% of the 10 m squares scored above 0.5. On 12 Mar 2026, in the dry season, it was 0.37 (18% above 0.5).

74% of the square got greener by more than 0.3 between the dry season and the monsoon. That is the pattern of one rain-fed crop a year, grown in the monsoon season. Seen by satellite

Village-wide view · first try · one-time, not updated

Paddy around the plots on 23 Oct 2026: how far along? Experimental

FarmBot tried to map paddy and its crop stage across the land within 2 km of the two plots (about 1,973 ha, more than the village itself), using the same paddy test as the neighbour comparison.

What it shows

On 23 Oct 2026, of about 272 ha of clear paddy found, about 269 ha was still green, 3 ha turning golden and 0.2 ha cut or bare. So the harvest around Nilabadi had not started yet, which matches the two followed plots.

What it can't show yet, honestly

It can't give a reliable Nilabadi total. The village boundary is not available, so the map can't be cut to the village. And the paddy test finds only about 59–70% of the two followed paddy plots, so it undercounts. For scale, the 2011 Census lists Nilabadi as 511 ha, with about 416 ha sown (all crops).
