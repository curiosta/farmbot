Official records

Nilabadi in numbers

These come from the 2011 Census of India village records and the government's village list (Local Government Directory), as shown on Odiapedia. They are from 2011 and may have changed.

2,123

people in 476 households (Census 2011).

511 ha

Village area: 511 hectares (about 1,263 acres). About 416 ha (about 1,028 acres) is sown with crops. About 173 ha (about 427 acres) gets canal water (Village Directory).

Paddy

is the main crop listed, then lemon grass and cotton. Village code 428775 in the government list. Neelabadi gram panchayat.

One-time satellite look · a 2.2 km square

Green now, brown in March

FarmBot looked at a 2.2 km square around the village. On 21 Oct 2026 the average greenness score was 0.77, and 96% of the 10 m squares scored above 0.5. On 12 Mar 2026, in the dry season, it was 0.37 (18% above 0.5).

74% of the square got greener by more than 0.3 between the dry season and the monsoon. That is the pattern of one rain-fed crop a year, grown in the monsoon season. Seen by satellite
