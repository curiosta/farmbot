In 2025 the pond fields peaked at 0.86 around 18 Oct and were mostly harvested by 27 Nov. In 2026 they greened more slowly than the temple plot (September median 0.61 against 0.73) and were still rising on 23 Oct.

Unlike the temple plot, these fields stayed moderately green (NDVI about 0.5–0.64) from late April into June 2026, before the kharif crop. Something was growing there: a summer crop, a nursery or weeds. That is worth confirming on the ground, because it shifts the "green-up" date above.

Inside the field · 10 m pixels

Weak-zone check Experimental

Each pixel fully inside the outline is compared with the field median on the same date, over the clear dates of 1–23 October in 2025 and 2026. A real weak patch would sit low in both years.

Result: no weak zone

  • Pond-area field A (north-east, by the road): 85 pixels tested, season-to-season agreement (Spearman) -0.37, 3 pixel(s) in the bottom 20% both years (4%, against about 4% by chance).
  • Pond-area field B (south-west): 37 pixels tested, season-to-season agreement (Spearman) -0.04, 1 pixel(s) in the bottom 20% both years (3%, against about 4% by chance).

The low pixels in one year are not the low pixels in the other, so no weak zone is claimed. The pond fields are too small to drop an edge band, so many tested pixels still mix in bunds and neighbouring fields. The weak strip north of the pond in the 31 Oct snapshot lies outside these fields (about 250 m away, none of it inside), so it says nothing about them; it stays in the snapshot.

Pond water extent · Sentinel-2

The pond held water all dry season

The main fishing pond lies about 487 m from the fields. Open water within 150 m of its centre (NDWI > 0) stayed between 0.9 and 1.6 ha on every clear date from Nov 2025 to Jun 2026. MNDWI suggests it shrank from ~1.5 ha (Dec–Jan median) to ~1.0 ha (Mar–May median). That is surface area only, not depth or volume. Satellite-observed

Read with care

  • Outlines are hand-digitised, not surveyed. They were traced from marked map screenshots matched to high-resolution imagery (about 1–2 m match error). Field edges are good to roughly 5–10 m.
  • 10 m pixels. The two pond fields cover about 100 and 40 Sentinel-2 pixels, many of them mixed with bunds and neighbours, so within-field detail is indicative only.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear Sentinel-2 dates, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Soil and weather figures are modelled estimates, not measurements on these fields.
