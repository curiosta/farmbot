Temple plot at a glance

  • Crop now. Grain filling / ripening. On the last clear satellite photo (23 Oct) the crop was thick green, greener than the same week last year. No sign yet of the field turning golden.
  • This week. 29 Oct–4 Nov: keep an eye on blast (a weather check, not a pest count). No heavy rain in the forecast for this plot's spot. The forecast for the other plot, about 1.3 km away, has heavy rain on Wed 4 Nov and Thu 5 Nov; forecasts this close together often differ, so be ready. Action: keep the field outlets ready to open, and look at the neck just below each ear for a grey-brown ring (neck blast). Ask KVK Koraput before any spray. Pest alerts for this plot →
  • Harvest. Rough window 15 Nov – 5 Dec (estimate). This plot was planted earlier, so it is likely ready first (mid to late November). Start checking the grain from about 10 Nov; cut when 80–85% of grains are straw-coloured and hard.
  • Against last year and the neighbours. In early October the crop was greener than last year. Most paddy within 2 km was a little less green than last October. So against the neighbours this plot is above them: it did better than about 9 in 10 of them.
  • Weak patch. None found: no part of the plot was clearly less green in both years.
  • Water and a winter crop. No known winter water source next to this plot, so there is no winter-water estimate for it. A winter crop here would depend on leftover soil wetness after the paddy (to be confirmed).
  • Next steps. (1) Check for neck blast, and keep the outlets ready in case heavy rain comes. (2) From about 10 Nov check the grain every few days and line up harvest help; the drier days in the forecast are 10 Nov to 13 Nov.

Weekly advice · written 31 Oct 2026

This week: 29 Oct – 4 Nov 2026

Crop stage: grain filling / ripening. Watch: Blast. See the pest alerts for this plot for the reasons.

What to do this week

  • Harvest: not yet. The last clear satellite picture (23 Oct) still showed a thick green crop. Rough harvest window 15 Nov–05 Dec (estimate from last year plus radar planting dates; this plot was planted earlier than the Fishing Pond plot, so it is likely ready first). Start checking ears from about 10 Nov: harvest when 80–85% of grains are straw-coloured and hard.
  • Storms/wind: if plants fall over, tie 4–5 hills together so ears stay off the water (IMD advice).
  • Drier days in the forecast (under 2 mm rain a day), useful for field work: 10 Nov–13 Nov (4 days).
  • Keep an eye on Blast (watch): Walk the field and look at the neck just below the ear: a grey-brown ring there (neck blast) makes the ear go white and empty. Also look for eye-shaped spots with grey centres on leaves.
  • After harvest: dry grain in the sun to about 14% moisture before storing; 12% if keeping it as seed (NRRI advice).

Weather is a computer forecast (estimate). Pest levels come from the weather, not from insect counts. FarmBot gives no pesticide doses: ask KVK Koraput or the agriculture officer before any spraying.

Harvested or ripening?

Not harvested. On 23 Oct the plot was thick green across almost all of it. In 2025 it started ripening about 10 days before the Fishing Pond plot.

Radar shows the plot was flooded for planting around 12 Aug 2026 (±12 days). The short green spell in late April and early May 2026 was before that, so it was most likely grass, weeds or a seedling nursery, not this year's crop.
