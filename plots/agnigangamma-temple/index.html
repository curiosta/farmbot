Agnigangamma Temple plot

Paddy, traced from a marked map. Sentinel-2 imagery, NDVI maps, a crop calendar, a weak-zone check and modelled soil.

Where

The exact plot

Traced from the marked map screenshot. Each screenshot was matched to high-resolution imagery by its own features (roads, ponds, tree clumps), with a match error of about 1 m. Only pixels whose centre falls inside the outline feed the crop numbers.

Where it is. The block lies on the north side of the village road, opposite the Agnigangamma Temple (public place listing, plus code X768+5R7), which is on the south side. Its centre is about 167 m NNW of the temple. The marked map was rotated about 113°, so the map's "top" is really west. A stream with a tree line runs along the north-east edge. The earlier square boxes are kept in the 31 Oct snapshot (not updated): about three-quarters of this outline fell in the north-north-east box, and the true-west alternative is ruled out.

Harvested or ripening?

Not harvested. On 23 Oct the plot median was 0.87, with 100% of its pixels above 0.6. That is peak greenness. In 2025 its smoothed NDVI fell below 0.7 about 10 days before the pond fields.

The satellite also shows a short green spell in late April and early May 2026 (median up to about 0.57), cleared by mid-May. It could be a summer crop, weeds or a nursery; only someone on the ground can say.

Inside the field · 10 m pixels

Weak-zone check Experimental

Each pixel fully inside the outline is compared with the field median on the same date, over the clear dates of 1–23 October in 2025 and 2026. A real weak patch would sit low in both years.

Result: no weak zone

  • Temple plot (north of the road): 637 pixels tested, season-to-season agreement (Spearman) -0.32, 14 pixel(s) in the bottom 20% both years (2%, against about 4% by chance).

The low pixels in one year are not the low pixels in the other, so no weak zone is claimed.

Read with care

  • Outlines are hand-digitised, not surveyed. They were traced from marked map screenshots matched to high-resolution imagery (about 1–2 m match error). Field edges are good to roughly 5–10 m.
  • 10 m pixels. The two pond fields cover about 100 and 40 Sentinel-2 pixels, many of them mixed with bunds and neighbours, so within-field detail is indicative only.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear Sentinel-2 dates, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Soil and weather figures are modelled estimates, not measurements on these fields.
