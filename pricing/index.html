Paid plan

Your field followed

Send us your requirements and we'll get back to you with pricing.

Everything in the free plan, plus one field followed closely each week:

  • Its own plot page, with a short summary at the top
  • Weekly advice for that field
  • A pest alerts section for that field, by its crop stage
  • A harvest estimate (a rough window, not a promise)
  • Weak patches: parts of the field that look weaker than the rest
  • The weekly archive: a saved copy of every week's report

Send your requirements

This opens your own email app with a short message to info@curiosta.com. Add your village, a landmark near your field and the crop. No phone number is needed. Nothing is sent until you press send, and sending your requirements does not commit you to anything.

Please read first

  • Pest alerts are untested in Koraput. They use published weather rules and have not yet been checked against pest counts here. Ask KVK Koraput before spraying.
  • Satellite only, for now. Everything comes from satellite photos and computer weather estimates. Nobody visits the field, and there are no sensors in the field yet.
  • No guarantee of yield. The advice and the harvest estimate are a guide, not a promise of a better crop.
  • Nothing is charged or collected on this site. There is no payment and no form here. Pricing is sent by email after you share your requirements.
