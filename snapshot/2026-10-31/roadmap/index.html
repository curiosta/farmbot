Roadmap

What comes next

Pest alerts first, then weekly advice and relative yield, then the supporting workstreams. No dates are promised yet.

1 · In progress

Pest & disease alerts

Turn cited weather rules into weekly risk checks per plot, compare them with field scouting, and only then publish scores. Adds a leaf-wetness estimate and NDVI anomaly flags.

2 · Next

Weekly recommendations

A weekly per-plot note: crop stage, rain vs ET0, pest check, and links to the newest ICAR-NRRI and IMD Koraput advisories.

3 · Next

Relative yield index

Peak and integrated NDVI per season and plot, as a relative index. It becomes meaningful once a full 2026 season is on file.

A · Next

Harvest-timing heads-up

Watch the weekly NDVI decline from the peak, with 2025's crossing dates as a guide.

B · Next

Within-plot weak zones

Track 10 m pixels that lag across seasons, check them on the ground, and use them to place the first sensors.

C · Soon

Sentinel-1 radar

Flooding, waterlogging and transplanting dates through monsoon cloud, fed into the pest model.

D · Soon

Rabi water budget

Pond area, ET0 and dry-season rain give hectares of green gram, black gram or mustard per metre of usable depth. Depth must be measured.

E · Later

Village-field baseline

Compare each plot with neighbouring Nilabadi fields as the yield baseline. Arrives with the village-wide view.

Later

Sensors, traps & cameras

Weather station, leaf-wetness and soil-moisture sensors, a pond level gauge, and pheromone traps with camera counts, using the village's electricity and 4G.

Later

Village-wide view

Extend the crop map and calendar from two plots to all of Nilabadi.

Ongoing

Confirm plot boundaries

Confirm the temple plot location and mark both field boundaries so the boxes become real plots.
