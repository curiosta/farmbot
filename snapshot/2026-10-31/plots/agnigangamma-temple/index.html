Plot · Nilabadi (Neelabadi), Bandhugaon, Koraput

Agnigangamma Temple plot

Paddy. Sentinel-2 (European satellites that photograph the fields about every 5 days) imagery, greenness score (NDVI) maps, a crop calendar from greenness and computer-estimated soil.

Where

Approximate location

A 400 × 400 m box centred about 18.962 N, 83.268 E. A 2021 European land-cover map (ESA WorldCover) labels it as 64% cropland, 21% trees, 14% grassland and 0.1% water. Only crop-land squares feed the crop numbers.

Location to be confirmed. The Agnigangamma Temple is at about 18.960 N, 83.267 E (public place listing, map plus code X768+5R7). The reference map screenshot was rotated, so "left of the temple" on screen is roughly north-north-east on the ground. The analysis box is centred about 200 m north-north-east of the temple. A second box due west of the temple was also analysed.

Harvested or ripening?

Not harvested. On 23 Oct the crop-land middle value was 0.86, with 97% of crop-land squares above 0.6. That is peak greenness. In 2025 its smoothed greenness fell below 0.7 about 17 days, and below 0.5 about 9 days, before the pond plot. So it may be ready a little sooner this year too. The brown fields in older Google imagery are from the dry season.

Alternative true-west box: 2025 peak 0.79, latest 0.81 on 23 Oct, 79% cropland.

Read with care

  • Plot boxes are approximate. They are squares drawn around landmarks, not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear satellite photos, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Temple plot location is still to be confirmed. The reference screenshot was rotated, so the box sits north-north-east of the temple; a true-west alternative was also checked.
  • Soil and weather figures are computer-estimated estimates, not measurements on these fields.
