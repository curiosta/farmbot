Plot · Nilabadi (Neelabadi), Bandhugaon, Koraput

Nilabadi Fishing Pond plot

Paddy. Sentinel-2 (European satellites that photograph the fields about every 5 days) imagery, greenness score (NDVI) maps, a crop calendar from Greenness map, pond water extent and computer-estimated soil.

Where

Approximate location

A 600 × 600 m box centred about 18.947 N, 83.264 E. A 2021 European land-cover map (ESA WorldCover) labels it as 79% cropland, 11% trees, 8% grassland and 1.3% water. Only crop-land squares feed the crop numbers.

Approximate. The box is centred on the fishing pond at about 18.947 N, 83.264 E. The pond is a ~0.78 ha (about 1.9 acres) water body, found by matching a map screenshot against Sentinel-2. There is a cluster of smaller ponds 100–300 m to its north.

In 2025 this plot stayed green longest of the areas checked. It peaked at 0.86 around 21 Oct and was mostly harvested by 4 Dec. Its September 2026 median (0.59) was lower than the temple plot's (0.74), and it is now close to peak.

Pond water · from satellite photos

The pond held water all dry season

Open water within 150 m of the pond centre (water score (NDWI) > 0) stayed between 0.9 and 1.6 ha (about 2.2–4.0 acres) on every clear date from Nov 2025 to Jun 2026. The second water score (MNDWI) suggests it shrank from ~1.5 ha (Dec–Jan median) to ~1.0 ha (Mar–May median). That is surface area only, not depth or volume. Seen by satellite

Read with care

  • Plot boxes are approximate. They are squares drawn around landmarks, not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear satellite photos, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Temple plot location is still to be confirmed. The reference screenshot was rotated, so the box sits north-north-east of the temple; a true-west alternative was also checked.
  • Soil and weather figures are computer-estimated estimates, not measurements on these fields.
