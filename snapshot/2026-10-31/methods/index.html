How

Method

  • Plots. Square boxes around two landmarks: 600 m around the fishing pond and 400 m north-north-east of the Agnigangamma Temple. They are not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Crop series. For each clear Sentinel-2 date (1 Oct 2025 to 23 Oct 2026), FarmBot takes the median NDVI of cropland pixels in the box. A 20-day running median smooths it, and the peak, the drops below 0.7 and 0.5, the dry-season floor and the 50% green-up point are read off that curve.
  • Pond water. Pixels with NDWI > 0 or MNDWI > 0 within 150 m of the pond centre, converted to hectares. For this metric only cloud, shadow and snow are masked, because the scene classifier labels this pond as soil.
  • Locations. The pond was found by matching a map screenshot to Sentinel-2 water. The temple comes from a public place listing (plus code X768+5R7) checked against imagery and OpenStreetMap.
  • Refresh. The site is generated from the data folders by a build script, and can be rebuilt weekly after new scenes are fetched.
  • Insights (experimental). Relative yield: the median of each plot's cropland NDVI over clear dates (at least 60% clear) in the shared 1–23 October window, 2025 vs 2026. Harvest heads-up: the weekly change in the 20-day smoothed NDVI. Weak zones: each pixel's NDVI minus the plot median, as a median over clear dates in each October. The cut is the bottom 20% in both seasons, after removing a 20 m edge band next to non-cropland. Rabi budget: Nov 2025 – Feb 2026 Open-Meteo ET0 minus rain, against the November median pond area (MNDWI) per metre of depth.
  • Pest rules. These are taken unchanged from the cited ICAR-NCIPM, ICAR-NRRI and IRRI documents. They are not tuned or scored for these plots yet.

Read with care

  • Plot boxes are approximate. They are squares drawn around landmarks, not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear Sentinel-2 dates, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Temple plot location is still to be confirmed. The reference screenshot was rotated, so the box sits north-north-east of the temple; a true-west alternative was also checked.
  • Soil and weather figures are modelled estimates, not measurements on these fields.
