How

Method

  • Plots. Square boxes around two landmarks: 600 m around the fishing pond and 400 m north-north-east of the Agnigangamma Temple. They are not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Crop series. For each clear Sentinel-2 (European satellites that photograph the fields about every 5 days) date (1 Oct 2025 to 23 Oct 2026), FarmBot takes the median (middle value) greenness of crop-land squares in the box. A 20-day running median smooths it, and the peak, the drops below 0.7 and 0.5, the dry-season low and the point halfway to full green (50%) are read off that curve.
  • Pond water. Squares with water > 0 or second water > 0 within 150 m of the pond centre, converted to hectares (1 hectare ≈ 2.5 acres). For this metric only cloud, shadow and snow are removed, because the satellite's own sorter mislabels this pond as soil.
  • Locations. The pond was found by matching a map screenshot to Sentinel-2 water. The temple comes from a public place listing (map plus code X768+5R7) checked against imagery and OpenStreetMap.
  • Refresh. The site is generated from the data folders by a build script, and can be rebuilt weekly after new photos are downloaded.
  • Insights (experimental). Crop comparison: the median of each plot's crop-land greenness (NDVI) over clear dates (photos at least 60% cloud-free) in the shared 1–23 October window, 2025 vs 2026. Harvest heads-up: the weekly change in the 20-day smoothed greenness. Weak patches: each square's greenness minus the plot median, as a median over clear dates in each October. The cut is the bottom 20% in both seasons, after removing a 20 m edge strip next to land that is not crop land. Winter-crop budget: Nov 2025 – Feb 2026 Open-Meteo crop water need (ET0) minus rain, against the November median pond area (MNDWI) per metre of depth.
  • Pest rules. These are taken unchanged from the cited ICAR-NCIPM (India's national centre for pest management), ICAR-NRRI (National Rice Research Institute) and IRRI (International Rice Research Institute) documents. They are not tuned or scored for these plots yet.

Read with care

  • Plot boxes are approximate. They are squares drawn around landmarks, not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear satellite photos, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Temple plot location is still to be confirmed. The reference screenshot was rotated, so the box sits north-north-east of the temple; a true-west alternative was also checked.
  • Soil and weather figures are computer-estimated estimates, not measurements on these fields.
