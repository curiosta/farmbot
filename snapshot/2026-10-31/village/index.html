Nilabadi in numbers

Satellite snapshot · 2.2 km box

Green now, brown in March

Across a 2.2 km box around the village point, mean greenness score (NDVI) was 0.77 on 21 Oct 2026 (96% of squares above 0.5), against 0.37 on 12 Mar 2026 (18%). 74% of the box gains more than 0.3 greenness between the dry season and the monsoon. That is the signature of a single rain-fed monsoon (kharif) crop. Seen by satellite
