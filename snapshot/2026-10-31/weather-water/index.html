Weather & water

Rain, heat and the pond

Computer estimate weather for the weather-map square that covers the village, and how much water the fishing pond held through the dry season.

Last 13 months · all computer estimates

One wet season, a long dry one

NASA POWER (a free NASA weather estimate) puts June–October 2026 rain at about 1,402 mm, peaking in Sep 2026 (496 mm). 6 months had under 25 mm. The hottest month was Apr 2026 (average day high 38.7 °C). Open-Meteo (a free weather service) gives 1,708 mm rain over the period and the water that sun and wind pull from a crop (ET0) of about 1,599 mm a year. Estimate

Water · observed

Fishing pond surface water

The pond held between 0.9 and 1.6 ha (about 2.2–4.0 acres) of open water (water score (NDWI), within 150 m) on every clear date from Nov 2025 to Jun 2026. The second water score (MNDWI) suggests about a 33% shrink from Dec–Jan to Mar–May. Seen by satellite Dry-season water (6 months under 25 mm of rain) is what matters for any winter (rabi) crop.
