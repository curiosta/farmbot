What FarmBot will do

Three priorities, in order

FarmBot is a farm copilot for two paddy plots in Nilabadi. It reads free satellite imagery and modelled weather now, and will add sensors, traps and cameras on the fields. Everything is explained in plain words for the people who farm here.

Priority 1 · in progress

Pest & disease alerts

Weather-driven early warnings for stem borer, gall midge, leaf folder, planthopper, blast, sheath blight and bacterial blight. Every rule is cited from ICAR and IRRI sources. No risk scores are published until they're checked locally. →

Priority 2

Weekly recommendations

A short weekly note per plot: crop stage, water balance, pest risk and links to the latest ICAR-NRRI and IMD advisories. →

Priority 3 · experimental

Relative yield index

Peak and season-long NDVI compared across seasons and plots: a relative index, not tonnes. The first October 2025 vs 2026 check shows no clear difference yet. →

Read with care

  • Plot boxes are approximate. They are squares drawn around landmarks, not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear Sentinel-2 dates, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Temple plot location is still to be confirmed. The reference screenshot was rotated, so the box sits north-north-east of the temple; a true-west alternative was also checked.
  • Soil and weather figures are modelled estimates, not measurements on these fields.
