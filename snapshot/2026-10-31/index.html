A farm helper for two paddy plots in Nilabadi

FarmBot follows the Nilabadi Fishing Pond plot and the Agnigangamma Temple plot in Bandhugaon, Koraput, Odisha. It uses satellites now, adds ground sensors and cameras later, and has an AI helper that explains it all in plain words.

What FarmBot will do

Three jobs, most important first

FarmBot is a farm helper for two paddy plots in Nilabadi. It reads free satellite photos and computer-estimated weather now, and will add sensors, traps and cameras on the fields. Everything is explained in plain words for the people who farm here.

Priority 1 · in progress

Pest & disease alerts

Weather-based early warnings for stem borer, gall midge, leaf folder, planthopper, blast, sheath blight and bacterial blight. Every rule is cited from ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) and IRRI (International Rice Research Institute) sources. No risk scores are published until they're checked locally. →

Priority 2

Weekly recommendations

A short weekly note per plot: crop stage, water balance, pest risk and links to the latest ICAR-NRRI (National Rice Research Institute) and IMD (India Meteorological Department) advisories. →

Priority 3 · experimental

Crop comparison score

Peak and season-long greenness compared across seasons and plots: a comparison score, not tonnes of grain. The first October 2025 vs 2026 check shows no clear difference yet. →

Also planned

Harvest-timing heads-up

Weekly greenness score (NDVI) decline per plot. No ripening decline yet.

Weak patches in a plot

10 m squares that lag in both seasons. They also show where the first sensors should go.

Radar that sees through cloud (Sentinel-1)

Flooding, waterlogging and transplanting dates through monsoon cloud, fed into pest risk.

Winter-crop water budget

How much pulse or mustard the pond could water. Pond depth is still unknown.

Village baseline (later)

Each plot compared with neighbouring fields in the same season.

Read with care

  • Plot boxes are approximate. They are squares drawn around landmarks, not surveyed field boundaries.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear satellite photos, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Temple plot location is still to be confirmed. The reference screenshot was rotated, so the box sits north-north-east of the temple; a true-west alternative was also checked.
  • Soil and weather figures are computer-estimated estimates, not measurements on these fields.
