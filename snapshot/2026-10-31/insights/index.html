Insights

Recommendations, yield and water

Weekly advice, a relative yield index and five satellite workstreams. Early results are marked experimental.

Priority 2

Weekly recommendations

Each week FarmBot will write a short note per plot. It combines the crop stage from satellite, the pest-risk check, the rain and evapotranspiration balance, and the newest ICAR-NRRI and IMD agromet advisories for Koraput. Every line will cite its source, and anything modelled will say so.

Crop stage

From the NDVI curve now, from radar later. Shown on each plot page.

Water

Rain minus reference evapotranspiration (ET0) for the past and coming week Modelled, and pond water area Satellite-observed.

Pests and official advice

The pest-risk check, plus links to the matching advisory text. The note does not prescribe doses.

Priority 3

Relative yield index Experimental

Satellites can't weigh grain. What they can show is how green and dense the canopy was compared with other seasons and plots. FarmBot will use the season's peak NDVI and the season's summed (integrated) NDVI as a relative index, not tonnes.

What this does and doesn't show

  • The data start on 1 Oct 2025, so the only window both seasons share is 1–23 October, near the peak. That window is compared here, not the whole season.
  • The 2026 season isn't finished, so its peak and integrated NDVI aren't known yet.
  • Both ratios sit inside the date-to-date spread shown in brackets. Read them as no clear difference between 2025 and 2026 yet.
  • Full-season integrated NDVI becomes possible once a complete season is on file. Comparing against nearby village fields comes later (see below).

Workstream A

Harvest-timing heads-up Experimental

FarmBot watches the weekly change in each plot's smoothed NDVI. When it starts falling steadily after the peak, the crop is ripening. A drop below 0.7 and then 0.5 marked the 2025 harvest window.

Nilabadi Fishing Pond plot

In 2025 the smoothed NDVI fell below 0.7 on 27 Nov and below 0.5 on 4 Dec. Latest smoothed value: 0.82 on 23 Oct, +0.04 over the past week. No ripening decline yet. Satellite-observed

Agnigangamma Temple plot

In 2025 the smoothed NDVI fell below 0.7 on 10 Nov and below 0.5 on 25 Nov. Latest smoothed value: 0.87 on 23 Oct, +0.00 over the past week. No ripening decline yet. Satellite-observed

Last year's dates are a guide, not a forecast. Variety and transplanting date change the timing, and November cloud can delay the signal.

Workstream B

Weak zones inside a plot Experimental

For each 10 m pixel, FarmBot compares its NDVI with the plot's median on the same date, over the clear dates of 1–23 October in both 2025 and 2026. A pixel in the bottom 20% in both seasons is a candidate weak zone. Pixels within 20 m of water, trees or houses are dropped, because mixed pixels there look weak.

What it shows

Fishing Pond plot: one strip just north of the pond stays about 0.1 NDVI below the plot median in both seasons. It's a place to check on the ground: it could be a weak patch, a different crop, or a land-cover mislabel.

Temple plot: the patterns don't repeat between seasons (Spearman -0.02), so no weak zones are claimed. The plot location is also still to be confirmed.

Use for sensors: put the first soil-moisture probe in a consistent weak zone and one in a typical zone, so the pair shows what differs.

Workstream C

Sentinel-1 radar

Radar sees through monsoon cloud. Standing water on a paddy field darkens the radar backscatter, which then rises as the canopy grows. That gives flooding and waterlogging dates and an estimate of the transplanting date, during the July–August months that Sentinel-2 missed. These feed the crop-stage and humidity inputs of the pest-risk model. Method only for now; no radar has been processed yet.

Workstream D

Rabi water budget Experimental

Can the pond carry a low-water winter crop such as green gram, black gram or mustard? The budget compares the water the pond holds with the crop's need after winter rain.

1.30 ha

Median pond water area in November 2025 (10 clear dates, MNDWI). It was 0.99 ha in March–May 2026. Satellite-observed

471 − 20 mm

Reference ET0 minus rain, 1 Nov 2025 – 28 Feb 2026: a deficit of 451 mm. Modelled Open-Meteo.

≈ 2.9 ha per metre

Each metre of usable depth over 1.30 ha holds about 13,000 m³. At the reference rate, that covers about 2.9 ha of crop.

Big unknowns

  • Pond depth is unknown. The answer scales directly with usable depth, so it has to be measured, for example with a level sensor or a staff gauge.
  • The figure assumes the crop uses water at the reference ET0 rate. Crop factors for each crop, field losses, the pond's own evaporation and seepage, and fish needs are not yet included. Those losses will lower the hectares.
  • Whether the farmers want a rabi crop, and other water sources, are still to be confirmed.

Workstream E · later

Neighbouring fields as the baseline

Comparing a plot only with itself can't separate a bad season from a weak field. Later, FarmBot will compare each plot's peak and integrated NDVI with nearby Nilabadi cropland in the same season. That turns the relative yield index into "better or worse than the neighbours". This comes with the village-wide view.
