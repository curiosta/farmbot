One wet season, a long dry one

Water · observed

Fishing pond surface water

The pond held between 0.9 and 1.6 ha of open water (NDWI, within 150 m) on every clear date from Nov 2025 to Jun 2026. MNDWI suggests about a 33% shrink from Dec–Jan to Mar–May. Satellite-observed Dry-season water (6 months under 25 mm of rain) is what matters for any rabi crop.
