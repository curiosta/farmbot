Rain, heat and the pond

Computer weather estimates for the village area, and how much open water the Panchayat Pond had.

One wet season, then a long dry one

NASA's weather estimates (NASA POWER) put June–October 2026 rain at about 1,402 mm. The wettest month was Sep 2026, with 496 mm. 6 months had less than 25 mm of rain. The hottest month was Apr 2026, when the day high averaged 38.7 °C.

A second free weather service, Open-Meteo, gives 1,708 mm of rain over the same period. It also estimates that the sun and wind pull about 1,599 mm of water a year out of wet ground (what this means). Estimate

Water · seen by satellite

Open water in the Panchayat Pond

The small Panchayat Pond across the road from the Fishing Pond plot is the water source already used for that plot. Its open water was 0.25 ha on 23 Oct, at most 0.40 ha between 1 Oct 2025 and 23 Oct 2026, out of a 0.81 ha outline mostly under weeds (by the water score). The satellite can't see depth. Seen by satellite

6 months had under 25 mm of rain. So the water left in the pond in the dry months is what matters for any winter crop (winter crop water). The village fishing pond's water can't be used, so it is not tracked.
