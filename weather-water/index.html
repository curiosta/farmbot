Rain, heat and the pond

Computer weather estimates for the village area, and how much water the fishing pond held through the dry season.

Last 13 months · all estimates

One wet season, then a long dry one

NASA's weather estimates (NASA POWER) put June–October 2026 rain at about 1,402 mm. The wettest month was Sep 2026, with 496 mm. 6 months had less than 25 mm of rain. The hottest month was Apr 2026, when the day high averaged 38.7 °C.

A second free weather service, Open-Meteo, gives 1,708 mm of rain over the same period. It also estimates that the sun and wind pull about 1,599 mm of water a year out of wet ground (what this means). Estimate

Water · seen by satellite

How much water the fishing pond held

The pond held between 0.9 and 1.6 ha of open water (within 150 m of its centre, by the water score) on every cloud-free photo from Nov 2025 to Jun 2026. A second version of the score suggests it shrank by about 33% from Dec–Jan to Mar–May. Seen by satellite

6 months had under 25 mm of rain. So the pond's water in the dry months is what matters for any winter crop.
