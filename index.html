A farm copilot for two paddy plots in Nilabadi

FarmBot follows two paddy fields near the Nilabadi Fishing Pond and the Agnigangamma Temple plot in Bandhugaon, Koraput, Odisha. It uses satellites now, adds ground sensors and cameras later, and has an AI copilot to explain it all.

What FarmBot will do

Three priorities, in order

FarmBot is a farm copilot for two paddy plots in Nilabadi. It reads free satellite imagery and modelled weather now, and will add sensors, traps and cameras on the fields. Everything is explained in plain words for the people who farm here.

Priority 1 · in progress

Pest & disease alerts

Weather-driven early warnings for stem borer, gall midge, leaf folder, planthopper, blast, sheath blight and bacterial blight. Every rule is cited from ICAR and IRRI sources. No risk scores are published until they're checked locally. →

Priority 2

Weekly recommendations

A short weekly note per plot: crop stage, water balance, pest risk and links to the latest ICAR-NRRI and IMD advisories. →

Priority 3 · experimental

Relative yield index

Peak and season-long NDVI compared across seasons and plots: a relative index, not tonnes. On 1–23 October the pond fields were less green than in 2025 and the temple plot greener, but the 2026 crop is still greening. →

Read with care

  • Outlines are hand-digitised, not surveyed. They were traced from marked map screenshots matched to high-resolution imagery (about 1–2 m match error). Field edges are good to roughly 5–10 m.
  • 10 m pixels. The two pond fields cover about 100 and 40 Sentinel-2 pixels, many of them mixed with bunds and neighbours, so within-field detail is indicative only.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear Sentinel-2 dates, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Soil and weather figures are modelled estimates, not measurements on these fields.
What is refreshed. Only the two marked plots (pond fields and temple plot) are refreshed each week. The village page and the 31 Oct 2026 snapshot (village box, candidate boxes, pond weak strip) are one-time and not updated. A wider-area view of other fields or villages could later become a paid subscription for others; that is only a placeholder.
