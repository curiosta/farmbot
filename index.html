Nilabadi · Bandhugaon · Koraput

A farm helper for the paddy fields of Nilabadi

Weekly crop and pest news for the paddy fields of Nilabadi village, Bandhugaon, Koraput, Odisha. Today FarmBot follows two plots closely: the Fishing Pond plot and the Temple plot (by the Agnigangamma Temple). There is also a weekly look at paddy across the village. More fields will be added over time. It uses free satellite photos now; ground sensors and cameras come later. Everything is explained in plain words.
