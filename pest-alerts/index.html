This week in Nilabadi, by crop stage

Risk this week comes from the weather only. It tells you when to go and count in the field, not when to spray. The weather is much the same across Nilabadi's paddy fields, so the weather risk is shared. What changes from field to field is the crop's stage: some pests and diseases only do harm at certain stages. Find your crop's stage under "If your crop is…". Not sure which pest or disease it is? Spot the pest has real photos of all seven. High = the week shows all the weather signs (for the insects, 4 or 5 of 5): walk the field this week and look closely. Weather, then count, then ask

How the check works

Risk this week is explained at the top of this page. Each pest or disease has 3 to 5 weather signs it likes (listed under “Weather that raises the risk this week”). High means the week shows all of them (for the insects, 4 or 5 of 5), Watch means one fewer, and Low means fewer still.

A High or Watch risk this week means go and count, not spray. Every 3–5 days, check 20 hills: 4 hills at each of 5 spots, one in each corner and one in the middle [10].

Ask Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK Koraput) only if your count is above the “Ask Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK Koraput) if you count more than” number. Below it, no spray is needed.
