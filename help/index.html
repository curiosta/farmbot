Where to get help, most useful first

Public offices and helplines for a paddy farmer in Nilabadi, Bandhugaon. Last checked 31 Oct 2026 on the official pages linked under each entry.

1. KVK Koraput: ask about a pest, a disease or a sick patch, or ask for a field visit

  • What it is. Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Koraput is the farm science centre run by the Odisha agriculture university (OUAT) at Sunabeda. Its scientists advise farmers and visit fields: they report 75 visits to farmers' fields reaching 2,850 farmers.
  • How to reach it. Phone +91-7008930482, email kvk.koraput@ouat.ac.in, or go to the office: Post Box No-10, Sunabeda, Koraput 763002. Staff listed on the official page include Ms. Sunita Dandasena (Senior Scientist and Head), Mr. Binod Chandra Behera (Scientist, Agricultural Extension) and Mr. Sabek Kumar Hantal (Soil Science).
  • When it helps. Before any spraying, when FarmBot shows a pest or weak-patch warning, or when you see an insect or disease you don't know. Take a clear photo and a sample of the plant.

Source: KVK Koraput contact page · KVK Koraput staff page · KVK Koraput: field visits (PDF)

2. Soil testing and the Soil Health Card

  • What it is. A lab test of your field's soil. The Soil Health Card shows the soil's nutrients and suggests how much fertiliser to use. The government pays for each sample (the Centre gives ₹190 per sample). KVK Koraput has its own soil lab and reports testing about 500 samples a year; it also handed out Soil Health Cards on 5 Dec 2024.
  • How to reach it. Ask KVK Koraput (phone above) or the Bandhugaon agriculture office whether there is any charge and how to send a sample. To take a sample: after harvest or when no crop is standing, dig a V-shaped cut 15–20 cm deep at the 4 corners and the centre of the field and mix the soil.
  • When it helps. Right after the paddy harvest, before a winter crop or before buying fertiliser for next season.

Source: PIB: Soil Health Card scheme · KVK Koraput: soil lab (PDF) · KVK Koraput: Soil Health Card day (PDF) · Koraput district: agriculture contacts

3. PMFBY crop insurance (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

  • What it is. Government crop insurance for crop loss. For a winter crop, the last date to join is usually 31 Dec; check the exact date with your bank or PACS.
  • How to reach it. Join through your bank, the PACS (village cooperative), a Common Service Centre, or the PMFBY website. The name on the form must match your Aadhaar. Helpline 14447 (24×7).
  • When it helps. Before the last date for each season. If an insured crop is damaged, report it within 72 hours on 14447.

Source: PIB: PMFBY enrolment and helpline · PIB: report crop loss within 72 hours (PDF) · PMFBY website

4. Weather advice for farmers: GKMS bulletins and the Meghdoot app

  • What it is. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) publishes district farm-weather advice (Gramin Krishi Mausam Seva, GKMS) twice a week, on Tuesday and Friday. The Odisha bulletin was issued on 30 Oct 2026. The free Meghdoot app shows the same advice on a phone.
  • How to reach it. Install Meghdoot from the app store and choose Koraput, or read the Odisha bulletin and the IMD farm weather site.
  • When it helps. Before spraying, irrigating or harvesting: it says when rain is due.

Source: IMD Bhubaneswar farm weather bulletin (PDF) · Meghdoot app page · IMD agromet

5. riceXpert app (rice institute, Cuttack)

  • What it is. A free phone app from the National Rice Research Institute (ICAR-NRRI), Cuttack, with advice on growing rice.
  • How to reach it. Install it from Google Play.
  • When it helps. To look up rice growing advice on the phone.

Source: riceXpert on Google Play · NRRI: about riceXpert

6. Krushi Samrudhi helpline 155333 (Odisha)

  • What it is. The Odisha government's free farmer helpline. It also calls farmers with advice through the season.
  • How to reach it. Call 155333. The Krushak Odisha portal has the state farmer schemes.
  • When it helps. For questions about Odisha farm schemes, or for advice by phone.

Source: Krushi Samrudhi helpline · Krushak Odisha

7. Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551

  • What it is. The national free farmer phone line. In Odisha the calls are answered from Bhubaneswar, in Odia.
  • How to reach it. Call 1800-180-1551, 6 AM to 10 PM, all 7 days.
  • When it helps. For a quick answer on a crop problem when KVK can't be reached.

Source: Kisan Call Centre: about · PIB: Kisan Call Centres

8. KVK trainings and the growing guide

  • What it is. KVK Koraput runs short farmer trainings and publishes a growing guide (package of practices) that covers rice, black gram and mustard/toria. It has also tested a newer rice variety, OUAT Kalinga Rice-1, in Koraput.
  • How to reach it. Ask KVK Koraput (phone above) for the next training dates, or open the guide below.
  • When it helps. Before choosing a winter crop or a new rice variety.

Source: KVK Koraput: growing guide (PDF) · KVK Koraput: training calendar, Dec 2025 (PDF) · KVK Koraput: Kalinga Rice-1 test (PDF)
