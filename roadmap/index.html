Features

What FarmBot does

Weekly help for paddy farmers in Nilabadi: pest weather, crop progress, harvest timing and water, from free satellite photos and weather data. Each card says what it gives you and how ready it is.

Available now

Pest and disease alerts

Each week: is the weather right for stem borer pest, leaf folder pest, gall midge pest, brown planthopper pest, blast disease of paddy, sheath blight disease or bacterial leaf blight disease around Nilabadi? Shown by crop stage. Not yet checked against pest counts here, so look in the field and ask KVK Koraput before spraying.

Available now

Spot the pest

Real photos of the common paddy pests and diseases, with look-alikes and what to look for in the field.

Available now

This week in Nilabadi

How far along the paddy around the village is, the village pest risk and the weather outlook, each week.

Available now

Weekly advice

A short note each week for each plot followed closely: crop stage, pest weather, heavy rain and dry spells, with links to advice from KVK Koraput, IMD and NRRI.

Available now

Is this year's crop better or worse?

Compares how green a plot is this year with last year, so a slow field shows up early. A fair yield comparison comes only after harvest.

Available now

When to harvest

A rough harvest window for each plot followed closely, checked against new satellite photos each week.

Available now

Weak patches in a plot

Points to parts of a plot that look weaker than the rest, so you know where to walk and look.

Available now

Radar that sees through cloud

Radar works through monsoon cloud. It gives a rough planting date (give or take 12 days) for each plot followed closely.

Available now

Water for a winter crop

An estimate of how much green gram, black gram or mustard the Panchayat Pond could water after the paddy, at an assumed depth of 1.5 m (range 1–2 m). The depth is not measured yet.

Available now

Compare with neighbouring fields

How each plot followed closely compares with the paddy fields within 2 km.

Coming later

Sensors, traps and cameras

A rain gauge, a depth ruler for the Panchayat Pond, pest traps and an optional 4G camera, planned for the 2027 paddy season. A starter set costs about Rs 7,850–14,650.
