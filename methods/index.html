How

Method

  • Plots. Outlines traced from map screenshots on which the fields were marked: two fields near the fishing pond (1.04 and 0.47 ha) and one block north of the road opposite the Agnigangamma Temple (7.47 ha). Each screenshot was georeferenced by matching its features (SIFT keypoints, a rotation-scale-shift fit with outlier rejection) to Esri World Imagery, with a match error of about 1–2 m. The pond fields follow the visible bunds; the temple outline follows the hand-drawn ring, trimmed 4 m back from the OpenStreetMap road. They are not surveyed boundaries.
  • Crop series. For each clear Sentinel-2 date (1 Oct 2025 to 23 Oct 2026), FarmBot takes the median NDVI of the 10 m pixels whose centre lies inside each outline (any WorldCover tree, built-up or water pixels are dropped), on dates when at least 60% of those pixels are clear. A 20-day running median smooths it, and the peak, the drops below 0.7 and 0.5, the dry-season floor and the 50% green-up point are read off that curve.
  • Pond water. Pixels with NDWI > 0 or MNDWI > 0 within 150 m of the pond centre, converted to hectares. For this metric only cloud, shadow and snow are masked, because the scene classifier labels this pond as soil.
  • Locations. The pond was found by matching a map screenshot to Sentinel-2 water. The temple comes from a public place listing (plus code X768+5R7) checked against imagery and OpenStreetMap; the georeferenced screenshot puts its pin within about 6 m of that point.
  • Refresh. The site is generated from the data folders by a build script, and can be rebuilt weekly after new scenes are fetched.
  • Insights (experimental). Relative yield: the median of each plot's NDVI over clear dates (at least 60% clear) in the shared 1–23 October window, 2025 vs 2026. Harvest heads-up: the weekly change in the 20-day smoothed NDVI. Weak zones: each pixel's NDVI minus the plot median, as a median over clear dates in each October. Only pixels fully inside the outline are tested (the temple plot also loses a 10 m edge band; the pond fields are too small for that). The cut is the bottom 20% in both seasons. Rabi budget: Nov 2025 – Feb 2026 Open-Meteo ET0 minus rain, against the November median pond area (MNDWI) per metre of depth.
  • Pest rules. These are taken unchanged from the cited ICAR-NCIPM, ICAR-NRRI and IRRI documents. They are not tuned or scored for these plots yet.

Read with care

  • Outlines are hand-digitised, not surveyed. They were traced from marked map screenshots matched to high-resolution imagery (about 1–2 m match error). Field edges are good to roughly 5–10 m.
  • 10 m pixels. The two pond fields cover about 100 and 40 Sentinel-2 pixels, many of them mixed with bunds and neighbours, so within-field detail is indicative only.
  • Monsoon cloud gap. July–August had only one or two clear Sentinel-2 dates, so transplanting dates can't be pinned down.
  • Soil and weather figures are modelled estimates, not measurements on these fields.
